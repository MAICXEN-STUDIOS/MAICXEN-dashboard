⚡ How Hybrid USB-FOTA Works
• MAICXEN EDGE Native USB-FOTA: Streams the firmware binary at 921600 bps with CRC-16/CCITT verification directly into the secondary OTA slot with rollback protection.
• Legacy / Factory Blank ESP32: Automatically falls back to esptool-js Bootloader mode without erasing saved Wi-Fi or NVS settings.
